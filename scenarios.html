The day · 9:40 to 10:25 · Classroom situations

What would you do here?

The ten cases below are fictional classroom adaptations grounded in published research or reporting. Each source describes the evidence behind the dilemma, not a claim that the scenario itself occurred.

Learning · Knowing · Creating · Relating

  • Learning What learning matters and what experiences make it possible?
  • Knowing What counts as understanding, evidence or trustworthy knowledge?
  • Creating What is being made, and how do authorship, experimentation and voice matter?
  • Relating Who and what are affected? What responsibilities, permissions and relationships matter?

Across all four: What is the machine doing? What could we redesign?

How each case works

  1. Read the situation and the question to sit with. Talk first, and note your first response.
  2. If you like, pick a Copilot prompt or write your own. The case and your first response are already in it.
  3. Write your group’s response and add it to the shared board.

You don’t need a Copilot account to read the cases. Each case page prints on its own.
