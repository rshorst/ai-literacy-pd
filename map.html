AI map

Who makes what in AI

Companies train models. Apps and tools put those models in front of you. One model often sits behind many apps, so a chatbot, a school tool and a coding assistant can share the same engine. Read the map from left to right and select any name to see what it connects to. The ideas at the bottom explain how it all works.

Icons after a model show what it can produce. Icons after an app show what it can do. The second tab maps the other kinds of AI that rank, sort, listen and translate, mostly out of sight.

    What can it do?

    Most AI is not a chatbot. These models rank feeds, sort and score, turn speech into text, translate, and predict, often where nobody sees them. Each kind shows what it takes in and what it gives back.
